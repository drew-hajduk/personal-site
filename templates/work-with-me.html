What's not included

So we're both clear from the start, these aren't part of either level.

  • Acting as your head of product, or joining your team's regular meetings
  • Hands-on design work, such as wireframes, UI or prototypes
  • Writing or reviewing code
  • End-to-end audits of a whole product or HR system, unless agreed separately
  • Project management, such as running sprints, stand-ups or delivery plans
  • Line-managing or hiring your staff
  • Responsibility for delivery, timelines or defects
  • Legal, compliance or pay advice for a specific employer
  • Selling or representing your product
  • Work beyond the agreed time, which is quoted separately

Who does the design and development?

I provide product leadership and oversight rather than doing the work hands-on. The design and development are done in one of two ways.

  • Your own team. I brief your designers and developers and review their work against the plan, so the skills stay in your business.
  • Trusted partners. If you don't have a team, I can introduce designers and developers I've worked with and trust. They work with you directly and are responsible for their own delivery, with me providing the product oversight.

How it works

  • I take on a small number of clients each quarter, so I can give each one proper attention.
  • Each level has a three-month minimum, then runs monthly with one month's notice.
  • You can move between levels with a month's notice.
  • Prices are plus VAT.
  • My role is advisory and leadership. Responsibility for delivery stays with the people doing the design and development.
