Who does the design?

I lead the work rather than design it hands-on. The design itself is done in one of two ways.

  • Your own team. I direct your designers and developers, so the skills stay in your business.
  • Trusted partners. If you don't have a design team, I bring in designers I've worked with and trust, and lead them as one package.
