## FAQ

### What does Drew Hajduk do?
I'm a product designer and founder. I design pay and reward software for HR teams, and I founded and launched PAYgrade and PAYreview.

### Can you help me build HR software?
Yes. I work with teams planning a new HR, pay or reward product, and with companies improving one they already have. That can mean product strategy, UX and interface design, or taking a product from idea to launch.

### Why work with a designer who knows HR and reward?
Pay software has to handle job evaluation methods, grading structures, pay ranges and the way reward teams actually work. I've designed and launched products in this space, so we spend our time on your product, not on learning the domain.

### What are PAYgrade and PAYreview?
PAYgrade is a job evaluation and salary banding platform used by around 25 organisations. PAYreview is software for running pay reviews. I founded and launched both.

### Where are you based?
I'm UK-based, proudly up North.
