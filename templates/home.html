About

Good products come from good people.

I'm a product designer by trade, with 16 years in the design industry and years of running the businesses around it. These days I lead rather than design hands-on: I decide what gets built, how it should work, how it's positioned and how it reaches the people who need it. The best of it has come from working alongside talented teams and partners, and my job is to give them clear direction and the room to do great work.

Where I keep coming back

HR software, reward and pay systems.

Pay is one of the most important numbers in anyone's working life, yet the systems behind it are often hidden in spreadsheets that only one person understands. I'm interested in how job evaluation, grading and pay structures actually work, and in how good product design can make them clear, consistent and fair for the people using them and the people they affect.

It's why both of my products live in this space, and why I still enjoy digging into how organisations decide what a role is worth.
